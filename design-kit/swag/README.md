# ejmlabs Field collection

Merchandise design concepts in Sage, Forest, Ink, Paper, and Mist. The original ejmlabs symbol and custom wordmark remain the identifying artwork.

## The collection

| Item | Design direction |
| --- | --- |
| Field tee | Paper cotton, small Forest chest symbol, flowing back print |
| Studio crew | Forest crewneck, Paper full-logo screen print |
| Field fleece | Mist pile, Forest trim and chest pocket, Sage symbol |
| Orbit hoodie | Forest hoodie, Paper chest symbol, Sage Orbit back print |
| Field socks | Paper ribbing, Forest heel and toe, Sage cuff stripes |
| Studio frames | Smoked Forest acetate eyewear with a small temple symbol |
| Studio cap | Forest six-panel cap, Paper embroidery, Sage back strap |
| Rib beanie | Sage ribbing with a small Forest woven patch |
| Studio glass | Clear tumbler with a frosted etched symbol |

The crewneck supplies the sweater direction; fleece and hoodie have their own constructions. Eyewear and drinkware are both included as glasses options.

The mockups were made with the built-in image-generation tool using the original artwork and motif library as references. They show proposed appearance and materials. The exact generation prompts are saved in `PROMPTS.md`.

The boards show appearance concepts. Fine line counts, small details, and surface texture may differ from the masters. Use `PRODUCTION.md` and the sized SVGs to brief a supplier. The crewneck method is a Paper screen print; an embroidered appearance in the concept board does not change that specification.

## Supplier handoff

Start with `PRODUCTION.md` for proposed placements and process choices. `artwork/` contains eight transparent SVG masters with physical dimensions, exact original logo paths, and linework adjusted for merchandise scale. These are the artwork references for sampling. They are not embroidery stitch files, garment patterns, supplier production dielines, or a manufacturing order.

The Flow highball has its own 180 × 112 mm flat wrap reference. Its proposed glass size and seam gap are starting points. The supplier must confirm the actual circumference, taper, and printable area before sampling.

Use a physical sample to choose thread, ink, fabric, patch, knit, and etch details. Keep the symbol's internal gaps open. Prefer one decoration process per area and let material quality carry the rest.

For a compact first set, the Field fleece, Studio cap, and Field socks give the collection three distinct materials and decoration methods.
