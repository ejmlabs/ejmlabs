# Swag sampling plan

Proposed products, fabrics, placements, and processes. All dimensions are starting points for sampling, not approved manufacturing specifications. Sizes refer to artwork width unless stated otherwise. Left and right mean the wearer's left and right.

The SVGs in `artwork/` are exact vector masters. They are not stitch files, patch dielines, manufacturing patterns, or evidence that any product has been made. Preserve the original logo geometry throughout sampling.

The image boards show appearance concepts. Their line counts, texture, and small details can differ from the exact SVG masters. Use the plan and masters for sampling. The crewneck uses a screen print even if the board suggests an embroidered texture.

## Placement starting points

- Tee and hoodie chest: place the symbol's top about 90 mm below the high point shoulder, with its center about 80 mm to the left of the center front. Confirm on the actual size range.
- Tee back: center Flow and place its top about 80 mm below the back collar seam.
- Crewneck: center the complete logo and place its top about 140 mm below the front collar seam.
- Hoodie back: begin with Orbit's top about 160 mm below the back collar seam, then check how the resting hood covers it.
- Fleece: size the smooth pocket panel to fit the 50 × 48 mm patch with room for its seam. Do not place the patch across a pocket opening.
- Socks: start with the symbol centered 45 mm below the cuff. Confirm its shape when the sock is worn.
- Cap: start with the symbol's bottom 22 mm above the visor seam. Check the front seam and crown curve during the sew-out.
- Beanie: center the patch on the turned cuff. Leave at least 8 mm between the patch and each cuff edge.
- Glasses: keep the mark away from the rim, base radius, and any seam. Sunglasses need a flat temple area large enough for the mark and its clear space.
- Flow highball: begin with the wrap's top 25 mm below the rim. At the proposed 155 mm glass height, its bottom is 18 mm above the base. A nominal 66 mm diameter gives a 207.35 mm circumference, leaving about 27.35 mm unprinted at the seam. Confirm the actual glass measurements, taper, and safe print area with the supplier before placing the artwork.

## Clear space and process checks

Reserve at least one quarter of the symbol's width around it. A 32 mm symbol needs 8 mm on each side; a 24 mm symbol needs 6 mm; an 18 mm symbol needs 4.5 mm; a 6 mm symbol needs 1.5 mm. The proposed patch bodies include that room before their border and stitch details are developed.

The complete logo at 180 mm needs at least 14.5 mm around it. Do not stretch, redraw, crop, or simplify the custom logo to suit a process. If a knit, stitch, or etch sample loses its internal spaces, change the process or increase the mark's size.

Flow uses 0.930 mm strokes and Orbit uses 1.044 mm strokes at the supplied print sizes. These widths exceed the kit's 0.8 mm sampling target. Fabric, mesh, ink, and supplier capability can require a different line width. Keep the selected artwork proportional and review a physical strike-off before approving a production run.

The highball wrap uses 0.896 mm Flow strokes and an original complete logo at 30 mm wide. Leave the supplied clear space around that logo. Review the fine wordmark gaps, two-color registration, seam, and ink adhesion on an actual glass sample. Request the supplier's glass-specific production template before a run.

For embroidery, request stitch digitization and a sew-out on the actual fabric. For patches, request the supplier's weave sample, border construction, attachment method, and dieline. For the smallest marks on socks and sunglasses, inspect the sample at its final size.

Hex colors are design references. Approve physical ink, thread, yarn, fabric, and acetate swatches together under the same light. The cream Paper body, Forest trims, and Sage details should form a restrained set.

No supplier, price, order quantity, product safety specification, or production order is set by this plan.
