Questions split in two on ultrawide

Why does the text grow on big screens?

At 1920 px and up the whole kit scales together, so a 45 inch screen reads like a laptop one meter closer.

Does it change my laptop layout?

No. Under 1920 px the scale is 1 and the brand sizes are exactly as published.

Where does the column stop?

It steps from 1200 to 1360, 1520, 1760, 2000 and 2360 px.

What about notches?

The page, headers and footer keep clear of safe areas when the viewport meta tag has viewport-fit=cover.

Do hover effects matter on a phone?

They are switched off where there is no hover, so nothing sticks after a tap.

Is 200% text size supported?

Yes, down to a 320 px screen. Long words break only when they cannot fit.

tip

Reading text stays at about 68 characters per line, and lead text at 54, on every screen. Wider screens add columns and white space, not longer lines.

rules

Three habits for every page.

The full checklist, the class table and the tool are in web/RESPONSIVE.md.

  1. Set the viewport tag.

    Use width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover.

  2. Use the parts.

    Pages carry no CSS, so every size rule applies to them.

  3. Shoot it.

    Run scripts/shoot.mjs and fix any overflow before you ship.
