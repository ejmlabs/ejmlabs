motion system

Calm by default. Short and eased out.

Every part here is a named class with a fallback for no script and for reduced motion. This hero is the ejm-enter choreography.

See the tokens

tokens

Five durations, four easings, three distances.

Pick the shorter one when two fit. Arrivals decelerate, exits accelerate and are faster. Ambient motion stays at 32 seconds, linear.

ejm-reveal

Arrive once, as they scroll in.

Four variants: up, fade, scale and left. Each plays once, in 420 ms. Without script, or with reduced motion, they are simply there.

Up

Fades in with a 16 px rise. The default.

Fade

Opacity only. For text, figures and calls to action.

Scale

Grows from 0.96. For panels and images.

Left

Slides 16 px in from the start side.

ejm-stagger

Siblings, one after another.

Put the class on the container. Children follow in source order, 60 ms apart. These cards contain links, so they lift 2 px on hover.

ejm-count

Numbers count up once.

The final number is written in the page, so it is there without script and for screen readers. The count runs for 720 ms, when the figure scrolls into view.

ejm-typewriter and ejm-cursor

Terminal text types in steps.

One short line, a character at a time, 40 ms each. The caret blinks eight times and then rests; nothing here loops.

feedback

Every control answers.

Hover, press and tab through these. Nothing needs a class: the kit's own parts carry the feedback.

Disclosures

Does the answer animate?

Yes, where the browser supports it: the height and a fade over 240 ms. Elsewhere it opens at once.

pages

Scroll, navigate, switch theme.

The Sage line at the top of this page is ejm-scroll-progress. The header above shrinks its logo and gains a hairline: ejm-sticky-shrink. Both are CSS only, and both are off with reduced motion.

In browsers that support cross-document view transitions, the next two links cross-fade in 180 ms while the logo and nav stay in place. The same cross-fade serves the theme switch.
