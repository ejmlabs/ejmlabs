dividers

Two ways to end a section.

Flow carries level lines from the symbol crossbar. Ripple puts the whole symbol at the centre. Use one between large sections, never both, and never between small ones.

draw on

Lines that draw once.

Inline line art draws on the first time it scrolls into view, in about a second, and stays drawn. With reduced motion, or without script, the finished picture is just there.

Quiet on purpose.

  1. Decorative only.

    Art is hidden from assistive technology and never carries a message.

  2. One idea per page.

    A hero picture, one divider style, one large surface. Not all of them.

  3. Brand colors only.

    Every line is a palette color, some at reduced opacity.

Small experiments. Useful things.
