## Choices for our films

Use the story structure and attention control, not another brand's visual
identity. Keep title cards brief. Give genuine product footage the full canvas
during work, without a persistent outer header, footer or step label. Move the
camera when the viewer needs different evidence, then hold still long enough
to read it. End the task on proof before the product URL.

| Film | Task and evidence | Camera focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CLI | Two failing cases, the actual patch, then the same five tests passing. | Establish the terminal, enlarge the tool or patch, hold on the passing result. |
| IDE | Search finds the shared pricing calculation; its native graph connects quote and settlement callers to the discount code. | Establish Search, focus on the graph, then follow its nodes into the source. Keep the inferred-link label visible. |
| Harness | JavaScript runs real tools and closes normally; a fresh Python process reads the same saved events. | Establish the SDK invocation, show tool results, enlarge the matching journal readback. |

Do not imply that Edi ran tests or that the harness readback resumed execution
or recovered from a crash. Source notes and [export verification](VERIFICATION.md)
remain the authority for what our delivered footage proves.

## Review limits and media rights

The team inspected timed frames, not full continuous real-time playback. Audio
was not auditioned, so this review makes no claim about music, narration,
sound effects or beat sync. Pixels cannot identify production software or
establish whether reference UI was recorded or recreated.

References were retrieved publicly without authentication or browser cookies.
Reference pixels, recordings and audio stay outside this project and its
delivery archive. This note includes links and observations only. It changes
no product behavior or public user guide and does not publish a website.
