# Proposal

## Why

ejmlabs is a one-person lab, not a studio. The brand guide and current copy say "independent studio" and speak as "we", which overstates the setup. The owner decided on 2026-10-18 to use "lab" and an honest solo voice.

## What Changes

- Describe ejmlabs as "a one-person lab for small experiments, useful software, and open source tools" in READMEs, X profile copy, kit source copy and the ejm-ui docs and project taglines.
- Use "ejmlabs" as the subject. Remove "we", "our", "us" and "the team" where they mean ejmlabs.
- Make no claims about customers, clients, rates, availability or paid work.
- Recompute the X bio character counts.
- Keep the tagline "Small experiments. Useful things." Leave "Visual Studio" and "VS Code" alone.

Out of scope: LICENSE, the brand guide, `site/` (rebuilt elsewhere), `design-kit/projects/`, `apps/gallery/`, other openspec changes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `brand-voice`: How ejmlabs describes itself in copy.

## Impact

READMEs, `x-kit/`, `design-kit/social/x-kit/`, design-kit docs, and in ejm-ui its docs, project taglines and the carried-over x-kit copy with its PROVENANCE hashes.
