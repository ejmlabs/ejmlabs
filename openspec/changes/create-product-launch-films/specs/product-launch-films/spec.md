# Product launch films

## Purpose

Provide short, editable product films that demonstrate real work, retain source evidence, and arrive as checked files ready for public release.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Story review before production

The delivery SHALL first present a script and timed visual storyboard for each product. Each SHALL name its audience, product category, useful task, visible result, exact film copy and next action. Planned shots and capture gaps SHALL be marked. Further fixture implementation, capture and video rendering SHALL wait for the user's story approval.

#### Scenario: Review the three proposed stories

- **WHEN** the user opens the storyboard review
- **THEN** each story can be followed without a source README and no planned result is presented as completed capture evidence

#### Scenario: Await story approval

- **WHEN** the scripts and storyboards have been delivered but the user has not approved them
- **THEN** new fixture implementation, product capture and video rendering remain paused

### Requirement: Genuine product work

Each CLI, IDE and harness film SHALL show its actual product or native output performing a useful task. Source records SHALL name the captured revision, replay mode, tool results and file effects. Authored marketing-site simulations SHALL NOT serve as product footage.

#### Scenario: Check a completed task

- **WHEN** a film shows a successful edit, test or restored session
- **THEN** the source record contains the real file change, process result or durable session evidence that supports it

#### Scenario: Use local replay

- **WHEN** fixed model replies supply a repeatable capture
- **THEN** real product tools execute the work, the film visibly labels model replay, and accompanying source notes distinguish tool execution from independent model reasoning

### Requirement: Product values and contribution

The CLI film SHALL explain speed and shared resource limits for many managed agents. The IDE film SHALL show a person reading and following source. The harness film SHALL show a clear API, extensibility and scoped speed evidence. Every film SHALL identify the project as open source and invite people to help improve it.

#### Scenario: Review the product narratives

- **WHEN** a viewer finishes each film
- **THEN** the product's useful role and one invitation to participate are clear

### Requirement: Scoped performance claims

Every numerical performance claim SHALL name its measured workload and relevant host, phase and runtime scope. Configured capacity SHALL NOT be presented as measured throughput. Managed sessions SHALL be distinguished from executing local jobs. Native engine measurements SHALL NOT stand in for CLI or SDK host measurements.

#### Scenario: Show many managed agents

- **WHEN** the CLI film names a session count
- **THEN** actual active runs in one CLI instance support that count and work limits remain visible

#### Scenario: Show an engine benchmark

- **WHEN** the harness film shows a timing or memory result
- **THEN** the displayed scope distinguishes the local native workload from model latency and consuming SDK hosts

### Requirement: Readable short films

Each film SHALL last between 20 and 35 seconds, establish a concrete problem, show useful product work near the start, preserve the product's own visual interface, and use the canonical project identity. Editing SHALL use punchy pacing and upbeat lo-fi when audio is included, keep important text readable, show the useful outcome through actual product actions and results, and end with the product URL. Tool activity and counts alone SHALL NOT serve as the story or outcome.

#### Scenario: View a film without sound

- **WHEN** a viewer watches a muted film
- **THEN** the task, important product actions, result and project identity remain understandable

### Requirement: Portable final exports

The delivery SHALL include one finished H.264 MP4 at 1920 by 1080 and 30 frames per second for each product, with a poster and editable source. Each MP4 SHALL use a broadly supported pixel format, fast start, and a checked audio track when audio is included.

#### Scenario: Open an export

- **WHEN** a viewer plays a delivered MP4
- **THEN** the entire video decodes, covers the intended scenes, has the stated duration, and contains no missing media or render errors

### Requirement: Safe and reproducible capture

Captures SHALL use disposable workspaces and isolated product state. Final footage SHALL exclude personal data, credentials and unrelated windows. Delivery notes SHALL describe capture, render commands, asset licenses, known limits and final verification.

#### Scenario: Repeat a capture

- **WHEN** a contributor follows the supplied instructions with the recorded source and tools
- **THEN** the same useful task runs in isolated state without a paid provider account or personal credentials
