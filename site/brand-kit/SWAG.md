# ejmlabs artwork and concept previews

This kit contains eight SVG artwork masters, their `artwork/manifest.json`, and
three concept previews. Read `RIGHTS.md` and `LICENSE` before using the files.
The kit does not grant a new license to the artwork or marks.

## Included artwork

| File | Intended starting size |
| --- | --- |
| `symbol-forest-32mm.svg` | 32 mm wide |
| `symbol-paper-32mm.svg` | 32 mm wide |
| `symbol-sage-32mm.svg` | 32 mm wide |
| `full-logo-forest-180mm.svg` | 180 mm wide |
| `full-logo-paper-180mm.svg` | 180 mm wide |
| `flow-sage-280mm.svg` | 280 mm wide |
| `orbit-sage-240mm.svg` | 240 mm wide |
| `flow-highball-wrap-180x112mm.svg` | 180 by 112 mm canvas |

The SVGs preserve the approved source geometry. `manifest.json` records physical
sizes, colors and source hashes. Scale proportionally. Leave at least half the
symbol's height around a full logo and one quarter of its width around a
standalone symbol. The SVG artboards do not include that external clear space.

## Before making an item

These files are starting artwork for sampling, not approved supplier production
files. Ask the supplier to confirm the item, placement, color, method and minimum
line thickness. Approve a physical sample before ordering a run. Embroidery needs
a supplier's digitized stitch file and sew-out; this kit contains no DST or PES.

The highball wrap is a sampling reference, not a supplier dieline. It starts from
a nominal 400 ml glass, 66 mm outer diameter and 155 mm height. Confirm the actual
circumference, taper, printable area, ink registration and unprinted seam before
sampling. Do not treat the SVG canvas as a cut line.

## Concept previews

`mockups/apparel-collection.webp`, `mockups/accessories-collection.webp` and
`mockups/glassware.webp` are visual concepts. They do not show verified physical
samples, available merchandise or products for sale. Color and placement in a
preview are not manufacturing specifications.
